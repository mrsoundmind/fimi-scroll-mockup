Overview

What this project is, in plain terms.

Food is medicine treats food as part of care, prescribed, delivered, and measured like any other intervention. Accelerating Food is Medicine II builds on an earlier two-year project, taking the three approaches with the strongest clinical and economic evidence, medically tailored meals, produce prescriptions, and medically tailored groceries, and working to move them from promising pilots into everyday practice, so the health systems and policymakers who can scale them have the proof to act.

Medically tailored meals

Fully prepared, condition-specific meals for patients with complex, diet-sensitive illness.

Produce prescriptions

Clinician-issued prescriptions redeemable for fruits and vegetables.

Medically tailored groceries

Condition-appropriate groceries for patients to prepare at home.
